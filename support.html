Support

Help with the kennel desk

PedigreePro is for iPhone and iPad. Write to dogshowapps@gmail.com and include the dog, the screen, and what you expected to happen.

Restore Pro

Open Settings, then Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account that bought PedigreePro Pro. Monthly is $6.99 and Annual is $49.99. Manage or cancel the subscription in your App Store account settings.

A dog will not save

Free includes 5 dogs. At the limit, the home screen offers Pro. A registry search is not saved until you review the ancestors and confirm.

Pedigree or print

Free pedigrees run to 5 generations. Pro opens 6 and 7, and exports a PDF on US Letter or A4. The kennel name on the certificate comes from Settings.

iCloud

Records can sync through your private iCloud database. If sync is unavailable, the app keeps a local copy on the device.

Lock

Settings can require Face ID or the device passcode before the kennel opens.

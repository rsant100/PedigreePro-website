Kennel desk

Every dog, the line behind it, and the breeding ahead.

PedigreePro keeps profiles, pedigrees, pairings, heats, contracts, and litters in one place. Print a certificate with your kennel name when the record is ready.

Download on the App Store See Free and Pro

The desk

Built the way a registry writes a dog down, then the way a breeder plans the next litter.

Profiles

Call name, registered name, number, breed, sex, color, date of birth, breeder, owner, and a photo. Link the sire and the dam.

Pedigrees

One generation out to five. Pro extends that to seven and prints US Letter or A4 with your kennel name and optional photos.

Pairing studio

Put two dogs side by side and see shared ancestors. Pro adds the coefficient of inbreeding. Save the pairing as a breeding when you are ready.

Season

Planned breedings, heat cycles, stud contracts, litters, and reminders stay next to the dogs they belong to.

Import, then confirm

Search a registered name, walk the line, and review every ancestor before it is saved. Nothing is written until you confirm it.

Private on the phone

An optional app lock keeps the kennel private. Records can sync with iCloud, and the app keeps a local copy if sync is unavailable.

Before the tie

Compare the pairing, then keep the litter.

Shared ancestors are on the screen before you breed. The desk holds the heat, the contract, and the whelp date after you do.

On paper

A certificate with the kennel name on it.

Print US Letter or A4, with photos or names only. The letterhead uses the kennel name from Settings.

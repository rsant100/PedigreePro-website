Privacy policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026. This is the same policy shown in the app.

1. Overview

PedigreePro is designed to help you manage dog profiles, pedigrees, and breeding records. This policy explains what data is stored and how it is used.

2. Data you provide

You may enter dog profile data, photos, lineage, breeding records, reminders, and saved pedigrees. You control the content you store.

3. Purchases

PedigreePro uses Apple In-App Purchases and RevenueCat to manage subscriptions. We receive subscription status and entitlement information needed to unlock Pro features.

4. Cloud sync

If available, your app data can sync through your iCloud account using CloudKit private database storage. If cloud services are unavailable, data stays local on your device.

5. Analytics and tracking

PedigreePro does not use third-party advertising SDKs and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared only with service providers required for app functionality, such as Apple and RevenueCat.

7. Data retention and deletion

Your data remains until you delete it in the app or remove the app and associated iCloud data. Subscription records are retained by Apple and RevenueCat according to their policies.

8. Security

We use platform-provided security features. No system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

PedigreePro is not directed to children under 13.

10. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Significant changes will be reflected by updating the policy date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, email dogshowapps@gmail.com.
